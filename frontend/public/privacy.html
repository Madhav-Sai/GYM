myGym privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

myGym is a personal gym, nutrition and recovery tracker run by Madhav on a private server. It is not a public service and has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This page says what it stores, why, and how to have it removed.

What it stores

  • Your account: a profile name and a password (kept only as a one-way hash) or a passkey.
  • What you log: workouts, body weight and measurements, gym check-ins, notes, photos you add, and your settings.
  • If you connect Whoop: with your permission, and only the read access you approve on Whoop's page, myGym receives your recovery, sleep, day strain and steps, workouts, profile (first name) and body measurements (height, weight, maximum heart rate). It also keeps the access and refresh tokens Whoop gives it, stored encrypted.

How it is used

Only to show you your own information inside myGym and to work out guidance from it (for example a recovery colour or your weekly heart-rate zone minutes). Nothing is sold, rented, used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

Who else is involved

  • Whoop, as the source of the data you choose to connect.
  • The services that run the site: a hosting provider (Railway) where the server and its storage live, and Cloudflare for the domain name. They handle traffic and storage on the site's behalf and are not given your data for any other purpose.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data is kept until you delete it.
  • Disconnect Whoop in Settings, Integrations, Whoop. That asks Whoop to end the connection and deletes everything myGym pulled from Whoop and the stored tokens. You can also remove myGym's access from your Whoop account.
  • To delete your whole profile and its data, use the account deletion in Settings, or ask the operator below.
  • Backups made by the operator are deleted on the operator's normal schedule.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, sessions are signed, and Whoop tokens are encrypted at rest. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

myGym is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/Madhav-Sai/GYM/issues.
